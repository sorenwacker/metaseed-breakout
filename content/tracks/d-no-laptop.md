## Goal

Collect concrete friction points from participants' own work for the discussion.

## Prompts on the poster

- **Specify:** which standard do you use, and who owns it?
- **Capture:** where is metadata first written down - notebook, spreadsheet, instrument?
- **Validate:** when do you find out something is missing?
- **Share:** who needs the metadata that is not in your group?
- **Export:** which repository or system receives it?
- **Revise:** what would make you request a change to the standard, and where would you send it?

Use one colour for "works" and another for "breaks". The facilitator groups the notes before the discussion starts.

## Goal

Experience how a profile guides metadata entry, and what validation reports.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the hub through SRAM with your institutional account. If you cannot sign in, pair with someone who can.
2. Import the prepared Excel file into a new MIAPPE 1.2 dataset.
3. Open the validation report and fix the errors: a missing required field, a malformed date, and an observation unit that points to a study that does not exist.
4. Share the dataset with a neighbour as viewer.
5. Export it to Excel and download the DCAT catalogue record.

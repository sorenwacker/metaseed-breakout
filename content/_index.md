## Abstract

> Metadata standards such as MIAPPE and ISA say what to record, but each tool builds its own version of them, and the standards change separately from the data built on them. Metaseed-hub treats a standard as a versioned, machine-readable specification. From that one specification it generates entry forms, validation, sharing, and exports to repositories and FAIRDOM-SEEK. In this session we go through the cycle once: define a standard, capture metadata, validate, share, export, and revise the standard without breaking existing datasets. Participants then work hands-on as a researcher, data steward or developer. Bring a laptop if you want to try it, or come to discuss where metadata gets lost in your own workflow. No prior knowledge needed.

## What participants take away

- A worked example of one metadata standard going through the full cycle, including a version change.
- A list of friction points collected from the room.
- Suggestions for which standards should become profiles next.
- Contacts for a pilot, and GitHub issues filed from what came up.

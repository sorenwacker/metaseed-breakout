Choose three or four questions. Notes go on a shared board and are read back during the wrap-up.

1. Who maintains a profile after the project that funded it ends?
2. What counts as a breaking change to a standard, and who decides?
3. How should datasets created under an old profile version move to a new one?
4. Where does a tool like this fit next to FAIRDOM-SEEK, electronic lab notebooks and the repositories you use (Dataverse, Yoda, ENA)?
5. Which of the standards named in the opening should become profiles next?
